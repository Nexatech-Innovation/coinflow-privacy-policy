Privacy Policy

What TuneXa collects, why it collects it, and what you can do about it. Written to be read, not skimmed past.

Effective: 1 January 2026 Last updated: 1 January 2026 App ID: innv.nexatech.tunexa

The short version

  • Your microphone audio is not uploaded by the tuner, vocal range finder, beat detector, looper or chord analysis. It is processed on your device and discarded.
  • Music recognition is the one exception — it sends a short audio clip to an outside service to identify the song.
  • We store an account record (email, display name, Pro status, token balance) so your purchases follow you between devices.
  • We never see your card details. Payments go directly to our payment processor.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. Pro removes them.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time by emailing us.

This policy explains how Nexatech Innovation ("we", "us") handles information in the TuneXa mobile application ("TuneXa", "the app"). By using TuneXa you agree to what is described here. If you do not agree, please stop using the app.

2. Audio and the microphone

TuneXa is a listening app, so this section matters most. How your audio is treated depends on which tool you are using.

Processed on your device and discarded: the tuner, vocal range finder, beat detector, metronome, looper and chord analysis. Audio is analysed in memory as it arrives. It is not recorded to our servers, not stored by us, and not used to train anything.

Sent off your device: music recognition only. To identify a song, a short audio clip is transmitted to a third-party recognition service, which returns a match. This happens only when you deliberately start a recognition.

Looper recordings and any audio files you analyse remain in your device's own storage under your control. Deleting the app removes them.

3. Permissions and why each is needed

Microphone RECORD_AUDIO

Required for the tuner, vocal range finder, beat detector, looper and music recognition. Without it these tools cannot function.

Internet and network state INTERNET

Sign-in, syncing your Pro status and tokens, serving ads, processing payments, checking for updates and music recognition. Many tools work fully offline without it.

Storage and media READ_MEDIA_AUDIO

To open audio files you choose for chord and key analysis, and to save looper recordings. We only access files you pick.

Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS

To tell you about updates and account activity. You can turn these off in your device settings at any time.

Install packages REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES

Used only to install an app update you have chosen to download from within TuneXa.

6. How long we keep it

Account information is kept while your account exists. Delete your account and we remove your account record, entitlements and token balance from our database. Purchase records may be retained longer where financial or tax rules require it. Data held by the services in section 4 is kept according to their own schedules.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction — view and update your profile in the app, or ask us.
  • Deletion — email us to have your account and associated data removed.
  • Notifications — turn off in your device settings.
  • Ads — reset or opt out of personalised ads in your device's Google settings, or remove ads entirely with Pro.
  • Permissions — revoke microphone or storage access in your device settings. Features needing them will stop working.
  • Withdraw consent — stop using the app and delete your account.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or the NDPR. Contact us and we will honour them.

8. Security

Account data is stored in Google Firebase and protected in transit with encryption. Pro entitlements are cryptographically bound to your account so they cannot be transferred or forged. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

9. Children

TuneXa is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

The services we use operate globally, so your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Those providers maintain their own safeguards for such transfers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top of this page always reflects the current version, and significant changes will be announced in the app. Continuing to use TuneXa after an update means you accept the revised policy.
